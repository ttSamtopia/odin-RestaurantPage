Chopper's Café

We were a One Piece cafe themed around Chopper, were he lived a peaceful life in the real world.
Every dish and drink had a little bit of him in it.

Our curry rice was shaped like Chopper's face, and the pancake came loaded with his favorite treats.
We also used a lot of cotton candy.

When we were open

We were open from December 24, 2025 to February 1, 2026,
at BOX cafe&space on B2F of SHIBUYA109 in Tokyo.

Thank you to everyone who came by.
